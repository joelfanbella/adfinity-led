Financial Institutions

Banking and Insurance companies typically have a captive audience interacting within a controlled environment, making them more receptive to a wide range of products and service offers.

Far removed from the clutter of traditional promotional material, digital signages and solutions are the perfect ambient marketing media to showcase relevant content, promote employee interactions, and enhance the overall experience for customers.

The Adfinity Advantage

  • Decrease perceived dwell time for your clients
  • Collect feedback from customers on experience management
  • Create upsell & cross-sell opportunities
  • Expose employees to corporate communication programs
  • Expose receptive clients to new products and services

Healthcare

Healthcare Centers are the ideal environment for networked digital signage solutions. With high waiting room dwell times, medical providers can effectively communicate and educate patients while reducing clerical staff interruptions.

Providers can offer patient education, preventive medicine programming, testing information, and sponsored promotional segments.

The Adfinity Advantage

  • Decrease perceived dwell time in waiting rooms
  • Suitable for after-hours education training for employees
  • Use digital menu boards, brand posters, and touch screens to inform patients
  • Promote medical specialties, doctors, and facilities

Hospitality

Redefine the way you welcome guests. From entry to rooms, digital screens revolutionize your marketing strategy and make your guests' experience truly unique.

Adfinity provides end-to-end functionality for in-room displays, digital menu boards, flight information, and meeting room schedules.

The Adfinity Advantage

  • Promote your brand or extend customized welcome messages
  • Offer information on promotions and special offers
  • Use as wayfinders or digital information desks
  • Engage customers at strategic points to influence decision making
  • Drive digital menu boards and restaurant displays

Retail

Retail chains present the perfect opportunity for brands to communicate with shoppers. Strategically placed digital signages enhance brand recall and influence buying decisions.

Our age and gender recognition technology guesses shopper demographics to publish custom relevant content, creating sales lifts.

The Adfinity Advantage

  • Create sales uplift inside retail outlets
  • Promote special offers and promotional schemes
  • Create upsell and cross-sell opportunities
  • Engage customers at the exact point of decision
